# Macro Risk Gate — future experiment only

Question: does macro-risk information add incremental development OOS economic value
to a validated, frozen standalone Quant DCA timing model?

Prerequisites: credible standalone DCA result; admitted macro evidence; stable diagnostic
definition; separate human authorization and a predeclared experiment ledger entry.
Until then the dashboard is read-only. There is no WAIT/BUY_NOW/NO_EDGE override,
confidence multiplier, threshold/weight/position adjustment or feature injection.

A future experiment must compare the same securities, cash, initial dates, costs and
execution opportunities under standalone and augmented policies. Predefine allowable
intervention, limited search space, economic hurdle, inner selection protocol and
failure criteria. Preserve the approved DCA deadlines and no multi-month carry rule.
Report incremental EPI and opportunity cost, calibration, coverage/abstention changes,
regime/sector/region/year robustness and dependence on lower-quality data. Account for
the extra researcher degrees of freedom; complexity loses when economics are equivalent.

No thresholds or production policy are prescribed here. No experiment or backtest is
run, and the retrospective holdout is not an optimization resource. Any later accepted
change requires versioning, human review, freeze and prospective validation.
