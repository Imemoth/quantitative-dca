This means the present evidence does not close the contract, not that no provider
could ever do so. Provider rows U1–U7 and claims P01/P03/P08/P13/P18/P20/P30 in
provider-decision-matrix.csv and provider-evidence.json are the auditable basis.

Do not call the Sharadar master with a last-price cutoff and treat its result as
the historical active universe. Still-surviving securities would be excluded and
snapshot fields could remain contemporary. Instead, require a provider-supplied
historical seed or reconstruct the eligible cohort from dated original records,
then reconcile event changes and immutable identities. Neither exists here.

An exchange list is not historical membership. An issuer filing index is not a
security master: multiple classes/listings, IPOs, venue moves and ticker reuse need
explicit effective/known-at intervals. EU means eligible EU venues/jurisdictions,
not a blanket synonym for Europe, UK listings or US ADRs.

Frozen specification section 11 permits an **audited historical proxy universe**.
This remains a valid low-cost option if it includes failed/delisted names, records
its source population and dated eligibility, and quantifies exclusions by year,
region and event type. Today's survivors are not such a proxy. No proxy is admitted
or newly selected here; there is no measured historical denominator.

Cheapest next evidence: a small dated US and EU cohort seed with membership and
identity events from official archives, plus one terminal case in each region.
If that cannot be furnished economically, request a scoped historical export quote;
do not subscribe to a global database merely for convenience. No outreach sent.
