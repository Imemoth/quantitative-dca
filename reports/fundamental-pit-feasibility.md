The smallest meaningful proof must join original and subsequently revised reports
for one unchanged security identity. Retain period_end, fiscal period, filing
accession, publication/available timestamps, units/scale/reporting currency, fact
context and supersession links. Replaying an earlier prediction must recover the
earlier value without consulting the restated one. A filing date alone is not
permission to use the numbers at that day's market open.

Normalization covers revenue, EPS, cash flow, margins, balance-sheet values,
shares and debt used by the frozen registry. It must handle duration versus instant
contexts, diluted versus basic shares, stock splits, currency and amendments.
Bank/insurer-inapplicable ratios remain structurally missing under the existing
applicability masks. Those masks do not excuse an entirely absent EU family.

Cheapest path: original filings plus a narrow, independently checked normalization
adapter. Its labour cost and EU archive completeness are unmeasured, so do not call
it a proven free full panel. A paid US normalization pilot does not solve the EU
archive problem or automatically satisfy publication/retention rights.
